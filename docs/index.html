02 — VINTAGE SOUND ENGINES

TEXTURE, BY CHOICE.

THREE CHARACTERS.
ONE MACHINE.

Three distinct vintage-inspired sampling characters. Bring classic digital texture and character to your sound.

CHARACTER / 01

A little rough around the edges.

A grainier digital texture for drums, chopped phrases and sounds that want a little less polish.

CHARACTER / 02

Warmth with a different weight.

A distinct vintage-inspired character. A different way to shape the feel of a bass phrase, a chord or a drum hit.

CHARACTER / 03

Texture with room for detail.

A more focused sampling character for when you want a different finish without giving up the identity of the source.

Choose per pad. Keep your original source.
Vintage-inspired characters, not claims of identical hardware emulation.

Capture audio from supported apps on both older and newer iPhones. Save it, chop it, and flip it into your next beat.

CRATE

PRO

One library for GØ GRAB, USB takes and scratch recordings. Search, preview and trim your audio. Load it into SAMPLER for chopping, or into a TURNTABLE slot for your next cut.

USB SAMPLING

PRO

Connect a compatible USB audio input by USB-C or a Lightning USB host adapter. Check the input, press REC, then STOP → CRATE. Keep the take in your sample library and use it in your next beat.

Capture only audio you own or have permission to use. Protected content and source apps may restrict recording. USB audio compatibility varies; USB audio is not MIDI.
