01 / SEE THE MACHINE

HANDS ON.
HEAD NOD.

Pads, knobs, swing and a sequencer. A hardware-inspired workflow, wherever the beat finds you.

Build the groove.
Play the details.

Build four patterns with 16 or 32 steps, then arrange them in SONG. Double-tap an active step to cycle its velocity. Add swing and shift each pad up to 100 ms early or late with GROOVE.

Drag a note’s right edge to set its length on a GATE pad. Tap a track number to select the pad silently, or drag across track numbers to select several for SOLO or MUTE.

02 / SAMPLER · GØ GRAB · CRATE

SAMPLE FROM
OTHER APPS.

Capture an idea with GØ GRAB. Keep it in CRATE. Cut it into your next beat.

AUTO DETECT · GRID · LIVE CHOP

16 / 32 STEPS · PATTERN / SONG

Load a file or record with the microphone. Find cuts with AUTO DETECT, divide the waveform with GRID, or tap in your own cuts with LIVE CHOP. Audition a slice on the waveform, then use ASSIGN TO PADS.

GØ GRAB

PRO

Keep up to the last 30 seconds made available by iOS. On older and newer iPhones, the app chooses a compatible capture method.

CRATE

PRO

Organize takes into folders and mark favourites. Filter by the original source, even when a take is inside a folder. Preview, trim and load into SAMPLER, SAMPLED or a TURNTABLE slot.

GØ GRAB and CRATE require PRO. Capture only audio you own or have permission to use. Protected content and source apps may restrict recording.

05 / BANK B · BASS & INSTRUMENT

BRING THE
LOW END.

Build a bassline. Find a melody. Make the whole beat yours.

Play a synth.
Or play your own sample.

B1–B8 play bass and B9–B16 play instruments. Choose a SYNTH preset, or load one sound in SAMPLED and play it across the eight pads. Trim the waveform, set the root note and octave, and choose ONE SHOT or GATE.

SMART KEY

Smart Key refreshes its key detection when the samples on A9–A16 change.

FLIP IT. RESAMPLE IT.

The performance
becomes the sample.

Print a pattern, song or live pass with performance FX. In TAPES, browse your takes, trim on the waveform and export WAV or send the recording to a pad. Choose A1–A16 or RES. Green lights show free pads and yellow lights show occupied pads. Use RESAMPLE in PAD EDIT to print just one pad.

Resampling & TAPES ↗

TIME STRETCH

Change length, keep the pitch. Drag the waveform handle or use FIT TO BPM. THIS PAD / ALL works within the current group of eight.

USB SAMPLING

PRO

Record compatible external audio by USB‑C or a Lightning USB host adapter. Check the input, REC, then STOP → CRATE. USB audio compatibility varies; it is separate from MIDI.

Explore the workflow ↗

MIDI LEARN
